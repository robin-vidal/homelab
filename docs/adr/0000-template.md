## Status

## Context
What problem or situation force this decision? What constraint exist
(hardware, time, prior choice)? No solution here, just situation.

## Decision
One or two sentence, what we choose to do.

## Rationale
Why this choice over alternative. List alternative considered if useful,
short.

## Consequences
What get harder, what get easier, what future-you must remember. Include
trade-off, not just benefit.
